# Homepage focus pillars — draft for sign-off

After combining the activities from your 2024–25 impact doc with the projects currently listed on the live site, I think four pillars cover the work cleanly and tell a strong impact story. Each one rolls up multiple programmes so the homepage stays simple while still doing justice to the breadth.

---

## 1. Health & Wellbeing
Care for the most vulnerable — from blood supply to clean water to cancer treatment.

- Howrah Blood Bank (2019 & 2023)
- Blood donation drives and thalassemia / transfusion programme (since 2021)
- Filter water coolers — Don Bosco School, Swami Narayan Temple (2025)
- Cancer treatment for a child (2025, via Lions)
- COVID-19 oxygen concentrators
- Manzil Centre for Disabilities

## 2. Education & Opportunity
Long-term scholarships and school support across three continents.

- DKM Scholarship for Social Work (since 2020)
- Uganda Education Scholar — Bwindi (since July 2023)
- Philippines Education Scholar — Nueva Ecija (Mar 2021 → graduated 7 May 2026)
- St Xavier's University, Kolkata — 8 students since 2021
- Adrija Das scholarship (2021–2024)
- Food for visually impaired children (2025, via Lions)
- Maheshwari School For All, Future Hope, Stepping Stone Day Care, Kailash Satyarthi Children's Foundation, Cricket Kit for Bal Ashram Boys

## 3. Community Relief
Showing up in moments of crisis and feeding those who feed cities.

- Dubai labour camp food distribution — ~4,300+ individuals across 18 camps
- Ramadan distribution to labour camps & mosques (4 consecutive years, since 2023)
- Taal Volcano Eruption Relief (Philippines)
- Pondicherry Tsunami, Bhuj Earthquake
- CRPF Soldiers in India
- Vatsalya Bhavan

## 4. Global Partnerships
Multiplying impact through trusted partners.

- $100,000 commitment to Lions Club philanthropy
- Father Redemptorist Foundation & Thai Red Cross
- Grassroots Business Fund
- Lift construction at Nausar Mata Temple

---

## How this lands on the homepage

The four pillars become the main "what we do" section — each one a card with a single hero photo, a one-line description, and a link through to a fuller page. Above them sits the impact-numbers band (lives touched, countries, years active), and below them a map highlighting geographic reach — India, UAE, Philippines, Uganda, Thailand.

If you'd rather collapse to three pillars (e.g. fold "Global Partnerships" into the footer as "Our partners"), or split one into two, easy to adjust — just let me know.
